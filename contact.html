Contact & FAQ

How do I report a lost item?
Go to the “Report Lost” page, fill in the details (title, category, location, date and your contact), and optionally add a photo. Submit the form. Your report will appear on the Browse page immediately.
How do I report a found item?
Same process as lost items — use the “Report Found” page instead. Be as specific as possible so the owner can recognise their belonging.
Is my contact information public?
Yes. The email or phone number you enter is shown on the item card so the other person can reach you. Use a college email or a number you are comfortable sharing.
Can I edit or delete a report?
Currently there is no public edit/delete. Contact the project team (details below) if you need a report removed or corrected.
What should I do after matching with someone?
Meet in a safe public place on campus (library entrance, canteen, etc.). Verify ownership before handing over the item. Once returned, you can ask the team to mark the report as resolved.
Who built this?
This is a college project for Shri Shivaji College of Arts, Commerce and Science — “SSC Vault”.
